3. Minimum Word Count for General Candidates

Candidates except Blind / Low Vision / LDCP who type less than 200 words for English Type Test in Ten minutes will be considered as Disqualified for this examination.

4. Minimum Word Count for PwD Candidates

Candidates belonging to Blind / Low Vision / LDCP who type less than 120 words for English Type Test in Ten minutes will be considered as Disqualified for this examination.

5. Nature of Mistakes & Deductions

  • a) Omission of words or insertion of foreign words will be considered as Major Mistake (-0.33 Marks).
  • b) Omission of punctuation marks and incorrect punctuation marks will be considered as Minor Mistake (-0.15 Marks).
  • c) Spelling mistake will be considered as Major Mistake (-0.33 Marks).
  • d) Insertion of punctuation at the beginning / inside / end of a word will be considered as Major Mistake (-0.33 Marks).
  • e) Letter(s) typed in small letter in place of capital letters and vice-versa at the beginning / inside / end of a word will be considered as Minor Mistake (-0.15 Marks).

6. Calculation of Marks for Accuracy

Marks = No. of Typed Words — (0.33 × Major Mistake) — (0.15 × Minor Mistake)

  • Qualifying Marks for Blind / LV & LD/CP (English): 84 Marks
